Roermond, in the Netherlands, features landmarks such as the Munsterkerk, Saint Christopher's Cathedral, and the Cuypershuis museum for travelers who visit to learn about its history. In 1342, local workers redirected the Meuse river to grant the town direct access to major European trade routes.
