Heerlen, in the Netherlands, is a city to visit for Roman, mining and street-art history, with things to see like Hoensbroek Castle, the Glaspaleis and the Nederlands Mijnmuseum. On 17 December 1965 the Dutch government announced in Heerlen's theatre that the mines would close, and the city later remade itself as the country's mural capital.
