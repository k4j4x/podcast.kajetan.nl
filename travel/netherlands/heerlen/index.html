English Heerlen Travel Guide & History | From Coal Mines to Street Art

Heerlen, in the Netherlands, is a place to visit for Roman and industrial history, featuring sights like Hoensbroek Castle, the Thermenmuseum, and the Glaspaleis. In 1965, the Dutch government announced the closure of the local coal mines, leading to a cultural transformation through street art and redevelopment.
