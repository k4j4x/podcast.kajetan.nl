English Emmen Travel Guide & History | Ancient Tombs and Modern Art

Emmen, in the Netherlands, features stone dolmens in the Emmerdennen forest, the Grote Kerk on the Marktplein, and art in Rensenpark. Visitors will find many things to see while exploring ancient history. In 1883, Vincent van Gogh lodged in nearby Nieuw-Amsterdam to paint the peat bogs.
