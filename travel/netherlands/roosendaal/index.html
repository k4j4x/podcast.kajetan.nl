English Roosendaal Travel Guide & History | From Peat Bogs to Railway Hub

Roosendaal, in the Netherlands, has centuries of history with key things to see around the Markt, such as the Sint Jan church and the old Raadhuis. In 1854, a new railway line to Antwerp opened, turning the medieval peat mining hub into an international transport junction.
