English Delft Travel Guide & History | Vermeer, Gunpowder and Microbes

Delft, in the Netherlands, is a compact city to visit for its canals, Delftware and the light Vermeer painted, with history on every corner and plenty of things to see. In 1584 William of Orange was shot in the Prinsenhof, now closed for renovation until about 2028, and in 1654 a gunpowder blast tore through the city at the height of its Golden Age.
