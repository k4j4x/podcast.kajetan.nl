Available in: English

English Amsterdam Travel Guide & History | An Empire Built on Mud

Amsterdam began as a muddy settlement behind a dam on the Amstel, and that history still shapes the things to see on a visit, from the canal rings to world-famous museums. In 1275, Count Floris V freed its residents from paying his tolls: a small privilege at the start of a long story.
