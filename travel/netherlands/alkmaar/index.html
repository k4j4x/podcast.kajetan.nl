Alkmaar, in the Netherlands, features historic canals, the medieval Waaggebouw, and the Grote Sint Laurenskerk for tourists who visit to explore local history. In 1573, residents smuggled secret messages in hollow vaulting poles to request dike breaches, flooding surrounding lands to break a Spanish siege.
