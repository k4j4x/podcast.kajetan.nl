French lines: stress lightly on the last syllable; (n) = don't say the n, say the vowel through your nose: ah(n), oh(n), and a(n) like the a of 'bad' but nasal; zh = the 's' of 'measure'; ay = a short 'ay' with no glide; eh = as in 'bed'; ew, oo, ur and uh as above; r = a soft throaty r, always pronounced.
