Greetings

Meeting someone for the first time? A handshake is the usual greeting in Belgium, at work and at a first meeting alike. With strangers, use the formal ‘you’ (u in Dutch, vous in French) and switch to je or tu once you’re on first-name terms.

Among friends and family a kiss on the cheek is common, but how many depends on the region. In Flanders it’s usually one kiss, with three for a celebration such as a birthday, and grown men tend to shake hands rather than kiss. In Wallonia it can be one kiss or three, and kisses between men are more common than in Flanders.

Not sure what to do? Let the other person lead; a friendly handshake is never wrong.
