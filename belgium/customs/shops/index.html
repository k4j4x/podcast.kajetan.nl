Shops & opening hours

In Brussels, for example, shops are usually open from 10:00 to 18:00, Monday to Saturday, and on the main shopping streets and in shopping centres some stay open until 19:00 or even 20:00. Since 13 August 2026 the rules are looser: shops may open any day of the week between 5:00 and 21:00, and the old compulsory weekly closing day is gone. These are limits, not obligations, so many shops simply keep their usual hours.

Sunday is still the quiet day. Shop staff generally may not work on Sunday afternoons, except in tourist areas such as the coast or cities like Bruges and Antwerp. In central Brussels, some independent shops in certain districts, such as the Marolles, do open on Sundays.

Need something late? Night shops (nachtwinkel in Dutch, magasin de nuit in French) may open between 18:00 and 7:00. Petrol station shops and newsagents follow their own rules.
