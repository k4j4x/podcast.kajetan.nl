Saint Nicholas

Long before Christmas, Belgian children have their own present day: Saint Nicholas, Sinterklaas in Dutch and Saint-Nicolas in French. On the evening of 5 December children put out their shoes, and on the morning of 6 December they wake up to find them filled with chocolate figures and coins, speculoos biscuits shaped like the saint, tangerines, marzipan and toys.

That timing is the main difference with the Netherlands, where the presents come on the evening of 5 December.

In the weeks before, the saint is everywhere: he visits schools, sports clubs, hospitals and shopping centres, and in Flanders his steamboat always arrives in Antwerp. In Wallonia he often travels with a donkey. And all over Belgium, speculoos in the shape of the saint is the treat of the season.
