Brielle

Zuid-Holland, Netherlands

English Brielle Travel Guide & History | When Rebels Sparked a Republic

Brielle, in the Netherlands, has a chalk night before April 1st, when teenagers draw on windows, just as rebel sympathizers once chalked the doors of Spanish loyalists. In 1572, storm-blown Sea Beggars smashed the town's north gate with a ship's mast and claimed it for William of Orange. Listen to the full episode for more stories and tips on what to see.
