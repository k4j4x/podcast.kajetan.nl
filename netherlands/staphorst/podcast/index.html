English Staphorst Travel Guide & History | Camera Bans and Open Doors

Staphorst, in the Netherlands, is a town where artisans hammer pins into small wooden blocks, dip them in bright paint and stamp colored dots by hand onto dark clothing. A municipal ordinance dating back to 1937 bans photographing the locals without their permission. Listen to the full episode for more stories and tips on what to see.
