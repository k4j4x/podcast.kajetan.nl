English Gorinchem Travel Guide & History | Holland's Finest Fortified Town

Gorinchem combines centuries of history with things to see on a visit, such as the Dalempoort, the Lingehaven, and the Grote Toren. In 2021, the ANWB voted it the most beautiful fortified town in the Netherlands for its preserved ramparts and historic monuments.
