Den Helder

Noord-Holland, Netherlands

Available in: English

English Den Helder Travel Guide & History | From Hell's Door to Gibraltar

Den Helder combines naval history with places to visit and things to see, including Fort Kijkduin, the Willemsoord shipyard, and the Marinemuseum. In 1811, Emperor Napoleon Bonaparte inspected the port and ordered the construction of Fort Kijkduin to build a defensive stronghold.
