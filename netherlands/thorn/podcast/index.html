English Thorn Travel Guide & History | Dodging Taxes With White Paint

Thorn features cobblestone streets, whitewashed houses near the Wijngaard, and the Gothic Abdijkerk among key things to see when you visit. In 1794, French revolutionary armies invaded the principality, causing impoverished residents to brick up windows and whitewash their walls to evade taxes.
