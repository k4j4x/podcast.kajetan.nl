English Zutphen Travel Guide & History | How a Medieval Town Saved Its Soul

Zutphen showcases medieval history along the IJssel river, where travelers visit sights like the Walburgiskerk, the Bourgonjetoren, and the Berkelpoort. In 1564, church wardens opened the Librije inside the Walburgiskerk to create a public reading room with chained books.

Nederlands Zutphen reisgids & geschiedenis | Van Vikingen tot Hanzestad
