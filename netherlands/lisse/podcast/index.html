English Lisse Travel Guide & History | How Mud Turned Into Tulips

Lisse draws travelers who visit for its bulb history, featuring sights like Keukenhof, the medieval tower 't Huys Dever, and Museum de Zwarte Tulp. In 1950, local growers opened Keukenhof as a spring exhibition, welcoming 236,000 visitors that year.
