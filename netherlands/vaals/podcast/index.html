Vaals

English Vaals Travel Guide & History | Where Four Borders Once Met

Vaals offers rich history and notable things to see when you visit, including the Drielandenpunt, De Kopermolen, and the historic Von Clermonthuis on Von Clermontplein. In 1839, the creation of Neutral Moresnet established a border point where four different territories met.
