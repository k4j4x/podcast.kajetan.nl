English Middelburg Travel Guide & History | Rising from the Wartime Ashes

Middelburg offers rich history and many things to see, including the Abdij, the Lange Jan tower, and the Stadhuis on the Markt. In May 1940, wartime fires destroyed over five hundred buildings in the center, prompting a fifteen-year reconstruction that rebuilt these landmarks.
