Enkhuizen

Noord-Holland, Netherlands

Available in: English

English Enkhuizen Travel Guide & History | Saved by an Economic Crash

Enkhuizen combines maritime history with notable things to see, such as the Drommedaris tower and the Zuiderzeemuseum. Between 1650 and 1850, economic decline shrank the population from 22,000 to 5,400, leaving its Golden Age buildings intact for future generations.
