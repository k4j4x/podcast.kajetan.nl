How to read the pronunciationRead it as English and stress the syllable in CAPITALS; always pronounce the r (lightly rolled or in the throat) and say w halfway between v and w. kh = the rasping 'ch' of Scottish 'loch' (Dutch g/ch); uh or u = a short weak 'uh' (so uhr = 'uh' + r); ai = 'eye', never 'ay' (ij/ei); ow = 'ow' as in 'now', but in OWT (ui) say it with rounded lips; ur = the vowel of 'fur' with rounded lips and no r at all (eu); ew = 'ee' said with rounded lips, like French 'tu' (uu); oo = 'oo' as in 'too' (oe).
  • Waar is …?Where is …?WAHR is …
  • Hoe kom ik bij …?How do I get to …?hoo KOM ik bai …
  • Waar is het station?Where is the station?wahr is ut stah-SHON
  • Waar is de wc?Where is the toilet?wahr is duh way-SAY'Het toilet' works too. Station toilets cost about €0.50–€1.10 (pay contactless); toilets on the train are free. In cafés they are meant for customers.

  • RechtdoorStraight aheadrekh-DOHR
  • Om de hoekAround the cornerom duh HOOK
  • Is het ver?Is it far?is ut VERYou may hear 'vlakbij' (close by) or 'een kwartiertje lopen' (a 15-minute walk).
