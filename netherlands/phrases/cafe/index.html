How to read the pronunciationRead it as English and stress the syllable in CAPITALS; always pronounce the r (lightly rolled or in the throat) and say w halfway between v and w. kh = the rasping 'ch' of Scottish 'loch' (Dutch g/ch); uh or u = a short weak 'uh' (so uhr = 'uh' + r); ai = 'eye', never 'ay' (ij/ei); ow = 'ow' as in 'now', but in OWT (ui) say it with rounded lips; ur = the vowel of 'fur' with rounded lips and no r at all (eu); ew = 'ee' said with rounded lips, like French 'tu' (uu); oo = 'oo' as in 'too' (oe).
  • Een tafeltje voor twee, graagA table for two, pleaseun TAH-ful-chuh vohr TWAY, khrahkhgraag means 'gladly' and is the most natural way to ask for something: 'Een cola, graag.'
  • Een biertje, alsjeblieftA beer, pleaseun BEER-chuh, ahl-shuh-BLEEFTGets you a small glass of draught lager (pils).
  • Een koffie, graagA coffee, pleaseun KOF-fee, khrahkhWant a latte? Ask for 'koffie verkeerd' ('wrong coffee').
  • Mag ik een glas kraanwater?Can I have a glass of tap water?mahkh ik un khlahs KRAHN-wah-tuhrDutch tap water is excellent, but restaurants don't always serve it for free.
  • Ik ben vegetarischI'm vegetarianik ben vay-khuh-TAH-ris
  • Ik ben allergisch voor …I'm allergic to …ik ben ah-LER-khis vohr …Fill in: noten (nuts), pinda's (peanuts), gluten, melk (milk).
  • Lekker!Delicious!LEK-kuhrTasty, but also 'nice' in general: lekker weer (nice weather), lekker slapen (sleep well).
  • Eet smakelijk!Enjoy your meal!ayt SMAH-kuh-luk
  • Proost!Cheers!PROHST
  • De rekening, graagThe bill, pleaseduh RAY-kuh-ning, khrahkhStaff rarely bring the bill unasked, and in busy cafés you often pay at the bar.

💡 Service is included: tipping isn't expected, but rounding up or about 5–10% for good service is appreciated.
