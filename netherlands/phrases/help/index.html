How to read the pronunciationRead it as English and stress the syllable in CAPITALS; always pronounce the r (lightly rolled or in the throat) and say w halfway between v and w. kh = the rasping 'ch' of Scottish 'loch' (Dutch g/ch); uh or u = a short weak 'uh' (so uhr = 'uh' + r); ai = 'eye', never 'ay' (ij/ei); ow = 'ow' as in 'now', but in OWT (ui) say it with rounded lips; ur = the vowel of 'fur' with rounded lips and no r at all (eu); ew = 'ee' said with rounded lips, like French 'tu' (uu); oo = 'oo' as in 'too' (oe).
