English Edam Travel Guide & History | No More Cheese Made Here

Edam combines local history with major things to see, including the Grote Kerk, the Speeltoren, and the Steenen Coopmanshuys. In 1778, the town built the Kaaswaag to weigh cheese, yet today not a single cheese farm remains in the town.
