Spiced cake (ontbijtkoek)

Ontbijtkoek

ont-BAIT-kook

Ontbijtkoek (‘breakfast cake’) is a dense, dark loaf made with rye flour, sugar or honey and spices such as cinnamon, cloves and nutmeg. Despite the name it isn’t only for breakfast: a slice, often buttered, is just as common as a snack or with coffee.

Deventer, nicknamed Koekstad (‘cake town’), is famous for its own version, the Deventer koek. The oldest known rules for the town’s cake bakers date from 1417, and the cake was shipped to Hanseatic cities as far away as Bergen in Norway. In the north, Groninger koek is a rye-and-syrup cake eaten mainly with coffee, sometimes with candied peel, ginger or nuts.

How to eat it

Cut a thick slice and spread it with butter, the classic way, or simply eat it plain with your coffee.
