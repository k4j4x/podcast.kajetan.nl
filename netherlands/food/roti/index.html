Roti

Roti

ROH-tee

Roti is the Surinamese favourite that has become part of Dutch eating. Strictly speaking, roti is the flatbread; in the Netherlands the word usually means the whole meal: a soft, thin flatbread (sometimes filled with split peas or potato) with curried chicken, potato, a boiled egg and kousenband (yardlong beans), all in a masala sauce.

The dish came to Suriname with Hindustani contract workers brought from India, and to the Netherlands with the large migration from Suriname in the 1970s, when it became a popular takeaway. You’ll find Surinamese places especially in Amsterdam, Rotterdam and The Hague.

Vegetarian versions and variants with prawns or aubergine are common too.

How to eat it

The flatbread usually comes folded on top. Tear off pieces with your fingers and use them to scoop up the curry, or just use a knife and fork.
