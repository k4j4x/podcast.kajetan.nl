Apple pie

Appeltaart

AHP-pul-tahrt

Dutch apple pie (appeltaart) has a buttery shortcrust base and sides, filled with apple pieces flavoured with cinnamon and lemon, often with raisins or currants. Bakers like slightly tart apples such as Goudreinet or Elstar. There are two classic styles: with a lattice of pastry strips on top, or with a crumble topping (appelkruimeltaart).

A slice of apple pie with coffee is part of Dutch culture and is on the menu almost everywhere: in cafés and restaurants, and as the classic break on a day of walking or cycling. It’s often served warm, all year round.

The standard extra is whipped cream (slagroom).

How to eat it

Use the little cake fork and work your way in from the tip of the slice, with a bit of cream on every bite. Don’t like cream? Just say ‘zonder slagroom’ (without cream).

How to order
