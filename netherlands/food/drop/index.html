Liquorice (drop)

Drop

DROP

Drop is Dutch liquorice: chewy black or dark-brown sweets made with extract of liquorice root, in countless shapes and flavours. It’s popular above all in the Netherlands, which is the EU’s largest liquorice maker; the Dutch eat around 32 million kilos a year, roughly 2 kilos per person.

The big divide is sweet (zoet) versus salty (zout). Salty drop gets its sharp kick from salmiak (ammonium chloride), and dubbelzout (double salt) is stronger still, a taste not everyone appreciates. There are also versions with honey, mint, aniseed or laurel.

You’ll find a whole aisle of it in any supermarket, and you can still buy sticks of dried liquorice root (zoethout) to chew on.

How to eat it

Start with a sweet one before you try dubbelzout. And go easy: eaten in large amounts, liquorice can raise your blood pressure.
