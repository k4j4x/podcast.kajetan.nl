Kinderdijk

Zuid-Holland, Netherlands

Available in: English

English Kinderdijk Travel Guide & History | How Windmills Beat the Water

Kinderdijk provides key things to see for those who visit to learn about Dutch water history along the canals of Overwaard and Nederwaard. In 1738, eight brick windmills were constructed along the Nederwaard to pump excess water into reservoirs and keep the Alblasserwaard polder dry.
