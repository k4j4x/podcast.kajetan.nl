Cycling & bike paths

Bikes rule Dutch cities. Bike paths are often red asphalt and separated from the road (no law requires red, it simply caught on). Treat them like a road: don’t stand or walk on them, and look both ways before crossing. Bikes come fast and quietly, and some paths run in both directions.

A ring of the bell isn’t rude. It just means ‘watch out, I’m coming’. Step aside and smile.

Renting a bike? Keep right, signal turns with your hand, stop at red lights, use lights after dark (that’s the law) and cross tram rails as close to a right angle as you can. Holding your phone while riding is illegal and can get you fined. Park in bike racks or a bicycle garage: in Amsterdam, badly parked bikes can be removed to the bicycle depot.
