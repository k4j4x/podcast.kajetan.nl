Greetings

Meeting someone for the first time? A firm handshake, eye contact and your name: that’s the classic Dutch introduction. People often introduce themselves rather than wait to be introduced, so don’t be surprised when someone at a party holds out a hand and simply says their name.

Then there are the famous three kisses on the cheek (left, right, left) between friends and family. They still exist, but honestly: they were already fading before the pandemic, younger people do it less, and since COVID many Dutch people say they’d happily drop them. A hug or a handshake is now just as normal.

Not sure what to do? Let the other person lead. Nobody will be offended by a friendly handshake.
