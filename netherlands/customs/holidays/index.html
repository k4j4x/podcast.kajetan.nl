King’s Day & holidays

King’s Day (Koningsdag) on 27 April, or the 26th when the 27th is a Sunday, turns the country orange. People dress in orange and the streets become one big flea market: on this day anyone may sell their second-hand stuff on the street without a permit (the vrijmarkt), within local rules on where and when. The party often starts the night before, on King’s Night. Expect big crowds, especially in Amsterdam.

4 May is very different: Remembrance Day (Dodenherdenking). At 20:00 the whole country observes two minutes of silence for the victims of war. Trains, trams and buses stop, and people stand still in the street. Please join in quietly. By law, shops close at 19:00 that evening, although a municipality may let them stay open. On 5 May, Liberation Day, there are festivals and events across the country.

On public holidays such as Easter, Ascension Day, Whitsun and Christmas, opening hours can differ, so check before you go.
