Shops & opening hours

Typical shop hours are about 9:00 to 18:00 on weekdays and until around 17:00 on Saturdays. Many smaller shops stay closed on Monday morning. Larger supermarkets usually open earlier and close later. In many places there’s one late-shopping evening (koopavond), usually Thursday or Friday, until about 21:00.

Sunday is the tricky one: each municipality decides whether shops may open. In some cities most shops open on Sunday afternoon; in other towns almost everything stays shut. Some shops, such as those at petrol stations and in hospitals, may open on Sundays everywhere. So check opening hours before planning a Sunday shopping trip.

Two more tips: bring your own shopping bag, as bags are rarely free, and don’t try to haggle. Prices are fixed.
