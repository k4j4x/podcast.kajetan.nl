Coffee & a cookie

Order a coffee in a Dutch café and there’s often a small biscuit or chocolate on the saucer. It’s known as het koekje bij de koffie, ‘the cookie with the coffee’, a small gesture of hospitality.

At someone’s home you’ll traditionally be offered one biscuit with your cup, not a plate piled high. That isn’t stinginess: the habit is often traced back to post-war thrift, and it suits a taste for simplicity and modesty. Take one, enjoy it, and wait to see if the tin comes round again.

Coffee is also social glue. At work, the coffee machine is the unofficial meeting point, with breaks around 10:00 and 15:00. ‘Bakkie doen?’ means ‘Shall we grab a coffee?’ The right answer is yes.
