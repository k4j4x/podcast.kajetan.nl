Borrel & gezelligheid

Gezellig is the Dutch word you’ll hear everywhere. It describes a warm, relaxed, together kind of feeling: a cosy café, a good evening with friends, a nice person. Its opposite, ongezellig, is not what you want anyone to say about your party.

A borrel is where gezelligheid (the noun) happens: informal drinks with snacks and lots of chat, often after work. The classic is the vrijmibo, short for vrijdagmiddagborrel, Friday-afternoon drinks with colleagues.

Borrel snacks (borrelhapjes) lean towards the fried: bitterballen (crispy balls with a creamy meat ragout inside; careful, they’re hot), kaasstengels (cheese sticks) and nuts. Invited? Just say ‘Gezellig!’ and join in.
