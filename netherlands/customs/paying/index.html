Paying & splitting

In the Netherlands you ‘pin’: pinnen is everyday Dutch for paying by card, and it’s how most people pay. Most places still accept cash, but some show a ‘PIN only’ sign. In a 2025 survey for the Dutch central bank, about 4% of shops were card-only, rising to roughly 9% in the big cities. Foreign credit cards work in more and more places, but not everywhere, so carry a debit card and a little cash.

Out with Dutch friends? Splitting the bill is normal. You can ask to pay separately (apart betalen), or one person pays and later sends a Tikkie: a payment request by app or message that you pay with a few taps. With over 10 million users, ‘I’ll send you a Tikkie’ is a phrase you will hear.
