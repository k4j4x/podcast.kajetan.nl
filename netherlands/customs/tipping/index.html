Tipping

Good news for your wallet: service has been included in Dutch prices for decades, so a tip is never compulsory and nobody will chase you down the street.

That said, a tip is appreciated when you’re happy. In restaurants many people round up, or leave around 5–10% for good service. In cafés and bars, rounding up or leaving a few coins is plenty. In a taxi you can round up if you like, and hotel staff don’t expect a tip.

Paying by card? More and more card terminals now suggest a tip, often 5, 10 or 15%. Pressing ‘no tip’ is completely normal: most people do. If the terminal doesn’t ask, tell the staff the total you’d like to pay, tip included, before they hand it to you. And if the service was just average, it’s perfectly fine to pay exactly what’s on the bill.
