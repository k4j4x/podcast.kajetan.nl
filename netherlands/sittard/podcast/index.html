Sittard

Limburg, Netherlands

English Sittard Travel Guide & History | When a Lion Walked Into Mass

Sittard, in the Netherlands, has a bronze lion at the door of St. Michael's Church for the circus lion that strolled into mass in August 1938. After a dysentery epidemic ended in 1668, the town promised to march every year to thank St. Rosa, and has kept that promise for over 350 years.
