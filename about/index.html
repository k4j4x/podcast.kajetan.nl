About Wandering Cities

Hi, I'm Kaj Wander. I live in Amsterdam, and I like travelling without much of a plan: a road trip, a new city every week. On one of those trips I was driving to smaller towns I knew almost nothing about, and I thought: can't I just listen to something about the place I'm driving to? There wasn't really anything. So I made it.

Wandering Cities is the quick travel guide for the towns and cities the big guidebooks give half a page. For every city you'll find:

  • a podcast episode of about twenty minutes, in six languages (English, Dutch, German, French, Spanish, Italian)
  • a map with the places from the episode
  • a story to read: the history and what to see, with sources (being added city by city)
  • basic phrases with pronunciation, local customs and food

It started with the Netherlands, because I can check everything there myself. Belgium is next.

How it's made

Wandering Cities is made with the help of AI. I come up with the ideas, choose and check; the podcasts, texts and drawings are made with AI tools. Every episode is built from a set of sources collected for that city: Wikipedia and Wikivoyage (CC BY-SA 4.0), and the official tourism, heritage and museum websites. After that, each episode is fact-checked against those sources, and episodes with mistakes are corrected and published again.

Found a mistake, or know something that belongs on the map? Mail me: kaj@wanderingcities.com

Why it's free

It's a hobby project that grew bigger, with no profit motive. AI and travel both have a footprint, so if Wandering Cities ever earns money, it goes to Trees for All.
